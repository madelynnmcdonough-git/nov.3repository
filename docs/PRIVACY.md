# Orbit privacy policy

Orbit analyzes data you voluntarily connect or import. URLs, titles, visit times, normalized order records, self-reported conversation text, catalog entries and feedback stay in memory in your dashboard tab. Reloading clears them. The extension does not store history. No analytics, backend, external AI, advertising tracker, passwords, cookies or credentials are used.

Chrome history requires explicit optional history permission in the extension popup and a dashboard analysis action for a selected 7/30/90-day window. The connector operates only at https://madelynnmcdonough-git.github.io/nov.3repository/ and local development ports 5173/5174 on localhost or 127.0.0.1 at that same path. It checks the sender's extension identity, top frame, origin and path. URLs lose credentials, queries and fragments before transfer. Recognizable search-result titles may still contain search text. Browser titles and URL paths can contain personal information; review before use.

The dashboard filters a conservative sensitive-topic vocabulary and does not infer health, religion, politics, sexuality or other sensitive traits. Keyword filtering cannot guarantee that all sensitive records are excluded. Delete unwanted records or sources, clear the session, and revoke Chrome permission from Connections & privacy. Other open tabs have separate sessions. Deletion does not delete Chrome's own history or imported files on disk. Revocation does not uninstall the extension.

Product records are supplied voluntarily. No retailer scraping or account access occurs. Imported prices are user-provided and unverified. External images are blocked until you choose to load one; the labeled image host then receives an HTTP request. Opening source links sends ordinary browser requests to those sites, which have independent policies.

Only application code/public assets belong on GitHub. Personal exports, profiles, imports, credentials and environment files must never be committed. The app does not persist raw records, but browser/OS memory, extensions, screenshots and your own export files are outside its control.

For software questions, use the repository's Issues page without including personal records: https://github.com/madelynnmcdonough-git/nov.3repository/issues . Store publication requires the publisher to provide and verify an appropriate support contact and review these disclosures before submission.
