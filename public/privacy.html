Your data. Your permission.

Orbit analyzes only activity you explicitly connect or import. Raw records and feedback stay in the current dashboard tab's memory. Reloading clears them. No analytics, cloud AI, backend, passwords, cookies, or credentials are used.

The optional Chrome connector requests history permission explicitly. It reads URL, title and actual visit-time evidence only when you choose a history period and start analysis. It operates only on the authorized Orbit dashboard path and documented local development addresses. URL credentials, queries and fragments are stripped. Search-result titles can still contain search text.

Sensitive keyword filtering is imperfect. Review and remove records you do not wish to analyze. The app does not infer sensitive traits. Interpretations are tentative, rule-based attention signals.

Delete individual records, remove sources, reset session data and revoke Chrome permission in Connections & privacy. Other tabs have separate sessions. These actions do not delete Chrome's own history, uninstall the extension, or remove your files on disk.

Product information is user-supplied and prices are unverified. External product images load only after a separate click; their hosts receive that request. Retailer links have independent privacy policies.
